# Optional sound flair

The three bundled `.ogg` files are original synthesized notification arpeggios,
generated by `tools/make_cues.py`. They contain no sampled recordings or Grateful
Dead melodies. Sound is off by default.

- `/gh sound builtin`: use WoW's built-in UI cue.
- `/gh sound custom`: use the bundled original musical cues.
- `/gh sound test`: preview the herb cue.
- `/gh sound off`: silence cues.

Herb loot, successful scanned Alchemy spell casts, and recorded cooldown
completion can trigger cues. Automatic cues are limited to one per eight seconds.
They use the SFX channel and respect the game's sound settings.

For different local clips, replace `herb.ogg`, `craft.ogg`, and `ready.ogg` with
short, compatible Ogg Vorbis files you created or are authorized to use. Restart
WoW after adding new files. Do not commit replacement recordings to the public
repository without redistribution permission. Purchasing a song or finding a
short excerpt online does not establish that permission.

Blizzard permits addon audio customization but explicitly warns against
unauthorized copyrighted audio distribution:
https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/ui-add-on-development-policy/24534

No Grateful Dead recordings are included or automatically downloaded. A future
licensed song pack would require permission covering the recordings and music.
